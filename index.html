BUM16 // PROTOCOL REFERENCE GUIDE

1. STANDARD 16-FSK (DEFAULT)

Modulates 4 bits per symbol onto 16 carrier tones spaced by 150 Hz. Requires relatively low SNR and is highly compatible across all sound devices. It is the base fallback protocol.

2. PARALLEL FSK (FAST MODE)

Transmits two carrier tones simultaneously (one on Channel A and one on Channel B, interleaved by 75 Hz). Modulates 8 bits (1 full byte) per symbol, effectively **doubling transmission speeds**. Recommended for quiet environments or direct tab capture.

3. FEC ROBUST (SECURE ERROR CORRECTION)

Transmits every symbol twice in succession. The receiver uses **Soft-Decision SNR Confidence Scoring** to choose the copy with the clearer Signal-to-Noise Ratio. Doubles transmission time, but survives severe ambient noise bursts and speaker echo.

4. AUTO-DETECT MODE

By default, the receiver monitors all three synchronization carriers (19000 Hz, 19150 Hz, and 19300 Hz) simultaneously. Whichever sync tone triggers first automatically switches BUM16's demodulator into that protocol.
